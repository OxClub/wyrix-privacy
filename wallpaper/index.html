Privacy Policy

Wallpaper HD 4K · Developer: WYRIX · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wallpaper HD 4K app (package com.wyrix.wallpaper, "the app") handles information. WYRIX ("we", "us") publishes the app. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. WYRIX does not run its own servers that store personal data about you.

2. Information processed by third parties

The app uses third-party services that may process limited technical data when you use it:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): shows ads. It may collect and use the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, OS version, app version), and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Pixabay: wallpapers are loaded from the Pixabay service. When you browse or search, your search terms, category selections and your device's IP address are sent to Pixabay to return images. See Pixabay's Privacy Policy. Images are provided by Pixabay and its contributors.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load wallpapers and ads.
  • Set wallpaper: to apply the image you choose as your home or lock screen wallpaper.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only): to save a wallpaper you choose to download to your device gallery. On newer Android versions the app does not request this permission.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

4. How we use information

Data is used only to run the app's features (browsing, searching, previewing, downloading and setting wallpapers) and to show ads. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data stored on your device

Wallpapers you download are saved on your device. The app may temporarily store cached search responses on your device to work faster. You can clear this by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data security

The app communicates with services over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data processing by the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. Because the app does not hold personal data, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
